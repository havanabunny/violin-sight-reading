💪 Muscle Anatomy

Learn the names first — then quiz yourself. Every muscle below appears in your program.

Back

Latissimus dorsi · "lats"

The big wing-shaped muscles on the sides of your back. Job: pulling movements — the prime mover in lat pulldowns and rows.

Trapezius · "traps"

The diamond-shaped muscle from your neck to the middle of your back. Job: shrugging, posture, steadying the shoulders.

Rhomboids

Small muscles between your shoulder blades. Job: pinching the shoulder blades together — worked hard in seated rows.

Erector spinae · "spinal erectors"

Two cords of muscle running along your spine. Job: keeping your torso upright — the target of reverse hypers and deadlifts.

Chest & shoulders

Pectoralis major · "pecs"

The chest. Job: pushing — the prime mover in chest presses and push-ups.

Deltoids · "delts"

The shoulder cap, with three heads: front, side, rear. Job: raising the arm — lateral raises hit the side head, shoulder presses hit front + side.

Arms

Biceps brachii · "biceps" — bi = two heads

Front of the upper arm. Job: bending the elbow — curls.

Triceps brachii · "triceps" — tri = three heads

Back of the upper arm (bigger than the biceps!). Job: straightening the elbow — pushdowns.

Forearms

Wrist flexors and extensors. Job: grip — dead hangs and plate pinches.

Legs

Quadriceps · "quads" — quad = four muscles

Front of the thigh. Job: straightening the knee — the prime mover in hack squats.

Hamstrings

Back of the thigh. Job: bending the knee and extending the hip.

Gluteus maximus · "glutes"

The buttocks — the strongest muscle in the body. Job: hip extension — the prime mover in hip thrusters.

Gastrocnemius · "calves"

Back of the lower leg. Job: rising onto your toes — calf raises.

Core

Rectus abdominis · "abs"

The six-pack muscle, front of the midsection. Job: trunk flexion — cable crunches.

Obliques

Sides of the waist. Job: rotating and side-bending the trunk.
