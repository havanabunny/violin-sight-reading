Key concepts

SAID principle

The body adapts to the Specific Ademands Imposed on it — D. Want endurance? Train endurance. There are no shortcuts around specificity.

Progressive overload

Gradually increase stress over time — more reps, more weight, less rest. Without it, the body has no reason to change.

Start with an assessment

NASM trainers assess movement first (e.g. the overhead squat) to spot compensations like knees caving in — then fix those before loading heavy. This is why Phase 1 exists.

Supersets live in Phases 2 & 5

Phase 2 pairs a stable strength move with a similar unstable one. Phase 5 pairs a heavy lift (1–5 reps) with an explosive move (8–10 reps).

Match phases to the goal

Fat loss → Phases 1, 2, 3. Building muscle → Phase 1, then cycle 2, 3, 4. Sports performance → Phases 1, 2, 5.
