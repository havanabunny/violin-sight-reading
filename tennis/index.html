🎾 Tennis

Technique, tactics & rules — learn, then quiz yourself.

Key concepts

Grips

Semi-western is the modern forehand grip — easy topspin. Continental (like holding a hammer) is for serves, volleys and slices. If your forehand flies long with no spin, your grip is probably too far toward eastern/continental.

Scoring

Points: 15 → 30 → 40 → game. Tied at 40–40 is deuce; win the next point for advantage, win two in a row to take the game. First to 6 games (by 2) wins the set; 6–6 goes to a tiebreak.

Split step

The little hop as your opponent strikes the ball. It loads your legs so you explode in any direction. No split step = always late.

Second-serve margin

First serve = pace + placement. Second serve = spin (kick or slice) for safety — a double fault hands the point away for free. Pros often win because their second serve doesn't break down.

Doubles: poaching

The net player crossing to intercept a ball headed for their partner. It pressures the returner and ends points fast — the single most effective doubles play at club level.
