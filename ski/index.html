⛷️ Ski

Technique, mountain sense & safety — learn, then quiz yourself.

Key concepts

Wedge → parallel

Everyone starts in a wedge ("pizza") — tips together, tails apart — to control speed. As you progress, the wedge narrows until your skis stay parallel through the whole turn. Rushing past the wedge is why beginners bomb hills they can't handle.

The fall line

The most direct path straight down the hill. Speed control = turning across the fall line. The more your skis point across the hill (not down it), the slower you go. To stop: turn fully across and dig in your edges.

Pressure the outside ski

In every turn, most of your weight belongs on the downhill (outside) ski. Beginners instinctively lean uphill onto the inside ski — and wash out. Trust the outside edge.

Trail ratings

🟢 Green circle = easiest · 🟦 Blue square = intermediate · ⬛ Black diamond = advanced · ⬛⬛ Double black = experts only. Ratings are relative to that resort — a black at a small hill ≠ a black at Jackson Hole.

Responsibility code

The downhill skier always has the right of way — it's your job to avoid the person below you. Also: don't stop where you're invisible from above, and look uphill before merging onto a trail.
